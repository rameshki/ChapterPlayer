## What the page shows (period switch: Week / Month / Year / All time)
Hero: listened time in the period, average per day, change vs the previous period. Tiles: books finished, current streak (consecutive local days with at least 1 minute listened), time saved by listening faster than 1x (book time minus wall time), longest day. Cards: "Every day" calendar heat grid (last ~14 weeks for Month/Year, scaled), "When you listen" weekday bars, "Most listened" top authors (and narrators, series: tabs or a small switch), "Where you listened" source split (Audiobookshelf / Plex by server type), "Your library" (books, total length, downloaded books and size, not started), **"Series finished"** (series whose every known book is finished; count + list, newest first) and **"Series to finish"** (series with at least one book finished or in progress and not all finished; each row "3 of 7 finished · 14 h left", sorted by fewest remaining; tap opens the series group). A merged book (several servers) counts once. Series come from the merged list's series metadata (any member that has it; ABS has it, Plex usually not). A "series" needs at least 2 books.
