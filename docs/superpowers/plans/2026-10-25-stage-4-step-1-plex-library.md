## The defect
`PlexLibrarySource.fetchBooks` calls `getLibraryAll` on a music section: a Plex music library lists ARTISTS at the top level (type 8), so the app imports artists (no author, duration, audio or chapters). Audiobooks in Plex music libraries are ALBUMS (type 9, author in `parentTitle`, cover in `thumb`, year, summary), the audio is in each album's tracks (`/library/metadata/{albumRatingKey}/children`), and chapters come from the track detail (`/library/metadata/{trackRatingKey}?includeChapters=1`), not the track list.
