# Widgets

The three Glance widgets, their states, sizes and cover handling.

Detail moved out of `CLAUDE.md` (review R8); keep it current when this area changes.

- Widgets (Glance 1.2.0, `shared/androidMain/.../ui/widgets`; receivers in the app manifest, provider XML and previews in `shared/src/androidMain/res`): Now Playing (narrow = play/pause, from 180 dp title + back 15 / play-pause / forward 30; the body opens the player through `open_player`) and Continue Listening. A widget's Play with nothing loaded (the Listen and Continue Listening Resume cards; Now Playing, which with nothing loaded offers the same last book through `nowPlayingWidgetState(state, book, items, online, downloadedOnly)` instead of "Nothing playing", "Nothing played yet" only without history) is HEADLESS: `WidgetPlayAction` (`ui/widgets/WidgetPlay.kt`, book id as an action parameter) runs `WidgetResumer.launchHeadless` in the app scope (one start at a time, only the book the widget offers or Home's Resume book, `widgetMayResume`) through `PlaybackStarter` (`resumeCopy`, downloaded first); MainActivity is not started, the widgets flip to their controls from the player state, a failure is a toast (`widgetStartReaction`); for Sign in again a notification (`PlaybackNotices`) opens the app (no activity is started from the background). A Play never replaces a loaded book (`widgetPlayDecision`, checked when the tap arrives since a card can be stale): a copy of the tapped entry plays on, another book stays ("Another book is playing", the widgets are redrawn). `WidgetPlayAction` waits for the start (`WidgetResumer.playFromWidget`, at most `WIDGET_START_WAIT_MS` = 20 s) so the widget's broadcast keeps the process's priority during the server calls; a longer start goes on in the app scope. The widget states share one merged-library flow (`WidgetStates(scope = app scope)`, dropped when unwatched) and Now Playing is collected only while some widget is placed. It relies on Android's documented widget-interaction exemption for starting a foreground service from the background; if Media3 still reports `onForegroundServiceStartNotAllowedException` (`PlaybackService` sets a `MediaSessionService.Listener`), the book is paused and `PlaybackNotices.keepListening` posts "Tap to keep listening" (channel `playback_notices`), which opens the player (`foregroundRefusalAction`). The last book is not preloaded at app start (no server session for a book nobody plays). The older activity path (`resume_book_id`, `shouldHandleWidgetResume`, opens the player after the start) stays for widgets drawn by an earlier version. The full player never says "Nothing is playing": `PlaybackStarter.status` (`domain/playback/StartStatus.kt`) gives `playerEmptyView`: a loading view while a start runs, the start's reason after a failure, and a player with nothing loaded and nothing starting closes itself (only when the player is the current route). A failure shows only for a start begun while the player's state was watched and for `START_FAILURE_SHOWN_MS` (30 s) (`visibleStartStatus`); Back, the system back and a successful start clear it. What they show is pure code in `domain/widgets` (`WidgetState.kt`, `WidgetCommands.kt`); `data/widgets` holds `WidgetStates` (the flows), `WidgetActions` (buttons act on the loaded book only, never load one; with nothing loaded the button opens the app) and `WidgetRefresher` (started by the application; redraws on state changes, not position ticks, Continue debounced) behind `WidgetHost` (`GlanceWidgetHost`). All three share one look (`WidgetUi.kt`: `cardModifier`, `WidgetPlayButton`, `WidgetCover`, `WidgetProgressBar`; sizes in `domain/widgets/WidgetLook.kt` `WidgetDimens`; colours in `ui/theme/WidgetPalette.kt`; design reference: the AudioBooth iOS widgets, our own Glance code): a rounded card (24 dp) drawn by the `widget_card_dark` / `widget_card_light` / `widget_card_system` (+ `drawable-night`) gradient drawables in the app's midnight theme (`WidgetPalette` is built from `DarkPalette` / `LightPalette`): dark = navy surfaceSecondary `#1E2742` to `#11131B` (halfway between surfaceElevated and background), the app's text colours, light side buttons, an amber play circle with the onAmber glyph; light = white to the warm paper surfaceSecondary `#EBE8E0`, dark text, the light amber `#8A5A00` with a white glyph; a thin progress bar (amber on a dim navy `#42485A` track; light: on a warm grey `#C1BBAF`); rounded-square covers (12 dp) with a placeholder square (the track colour) when there is none. Colours follow `AppSettings.themeMode` (`widgetCard`: "follow system" uses day/night providers and the night drawable from Android 12; before that Glance bakes the colours with the app's night mode, so the card is `widget_card_dark` / `_light` chosen from the same configuration, and `GlanceWidgetHost` redraws every widget on a day/night switch, `widgetsRedrawOnNightChange`). The picker previews (`previewLayout`) follow day/night through `values(-night)/widget_colors.xml` and `widget_card_system` (tested against the palette); the pre-12 `previewImage` vectors stay dark (navy). `WidgetPaletteContrastTest` checks both themes at both ends of the gradient, that the colours come from the app palette, and that the drawables and the pre-12 picker images carry them. Every widget shows the book's cover (Now Playing from 250 dp wide, `nowPlayingShowsCover`; Listen in every size but the mini bar). R8 keeps the `ActionCallback` constructors (`proguard-rules.pro`). A third widget, Listen (`ListenWidget`, `ListenWidgetReceiver`, `xml/listen_widget_info.xml`, placed 4x2), joins Resume and the controls: rules in `domain/widgets/ListenWidget.kt` (`listenWidgetState`: a loaded book, playing or paused, shows its controls with the Now Playing title and button rules; else the Continue Listening book through `continueWidgetState` itself, so both widgets offer the same copy; `Empty` says "Nothing played yet" or "Nothing to resume now"; the state moves at most once per whole percent, never on a tick). Sizes (`listenSize`, `listenLayout`): Glance lays the widget out only at the ten `LISTEN_BUCKETS` (`SizeMode.Responsive`; `LocalSize` is the bucket, never the launcher's size, and the launcher shows the closest bucket that fits), so every width rule is reached only through a bucket: MINI 110x40 and MINI_TALL 110x56 (play 40 / 48 dp and the title, no cover), BAR 190x68 (44 dp cover, title and the time left or chapter, play only: the title keeps 110 dp before back / forward may show) and BAR_ROOMY 340x68 (back 15 / forward 30 at 48 dp), SQUARE 110x160 (52 dp cover, title, play with the time left beside it), WIDE 200x160 (72 dp cover; title, author or chapter, progress, time left, play only), WIDE_MID 260x160 (92 dp cover, back / forward at 40 dp) and WIDE_ROOMY 330x160 (all five buttons at 40 dp), LARGE 200x250 (80 dp cover, two-line title, author and chapter, progress, back / forward at 48 dp) and LARGE_ROOMY 270x250 (112 dp cover, all five at 48 dp). On a phone: 2x1 MINI_TALL, 3x1 and 4x1 BAR, 2x2 SQUARE, 3x2 WIDE, 4x2 WIDE_MID, 3x3 LARGE, 4x3 LARGE_ROOMY, 4x1 sideways MINI, 4x2 sideways BAR_ROOMY, 4x3 sideways WIDE_ROOMY (the test models the launcher's choice apart from the production code). More buttons win over bigger ones; chapter buttons only with two chapters or more, skips only with a known length. `listenContentHeightDp` (sizes in `ListenDimens`) is tested to fit every bucket at font scale 1.0 and 1.3 and the buttons each bucket shows are checked against widths worked by hand; font scale 2.0 (Android 14) can clip the lowest buckets (known limit). Before Android 12 Glance ignores the progress bar's colours (the system tint shows). Play in the Resume state is the headless widget Play (`WidgetPlayAction`); the controls are `Listen*Action` callbacks through `WidgetActions` (`previousChapter` / `nextChapter` use `remoteAction`), which act on the loaded book only (`runWidgetButton`, shared with Now Playing); the body opens the player while a book is loaded. `WidgetRefresher.listen` is `WidgetStates.listen` computed once and shared (the refresher and the widget's session), only while a Listen widget is placed (`WidgetHost.listenPlaced` at start, then the receiver's onEnabled / onDisabled and every `provideGlance`); it redraws Listen at once for the controls and debounced like Continue for the Resume state, and asks `WidgetCovers.prefetch` for the shown book's cover off the redraw when the state changes, the network comes back or the widget is drawn (`requestCover`, from `provideGlance`: placed, resized, a system update); `coverNextStep` redraws once on a new cover and retries a failure after the loader's 60 s backoff while online, at most 3 tries. The refresher shares each widget's state flow once (redraws and covers), asks for the covers of the books all three show (a `NowPlayingWidgetState.Book` carries its `bookId`) only while some widget is placed (`anyPlaced`: `WidgetHost.anyPlaced` at start, a drawn widget's `requestCover`, the receivers' `onDisabled` via `widgetsRemoved`), and draws all three again when one arrives (Listen only while placed) in its own coroutine, so a request arriving meanwhile cannot cut the redraws short. Android's `WidgetCoverCache` (`ui/widgets`, was `ListenCoverCache`) loads covers in the app through `CoverBytesLoader` (downloaded cover first, token only in a header), decodes them as RGB_565 to at most 256 px (`WIDGET_COVER_MAX_SIDE`, `AndroidCoverScaler.decodeScaledBitmap`; 128 KiB per cover; the eight cover buckets draw the same Bitmap, which RemoteViews' shared bitmap cache sends once per update) and keeps the 3 used last, by access (at most 384 KiB); the widgets get a bitmap, never an address. R8 renames `ListenWidget` (like the two older widget classes; no keepnames rule, nothing shows it causes harm: the system sends APPWIDGET_UPDATE after a package update). Host-tested only
