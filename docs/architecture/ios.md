# iOS

Everything iOS-specific: the app shell, platform parts, playback and downloads on iOS (compiled and CI-linked only; never run on a device).

Detail moved out of `CLAUDE.md` (review R8); keep it current when this area changes.

- Three parts: `shared/` (Kotlin Multiplatform, Android + iOS targets; all app code), `androidApp/` (activity, manifest) and `iosApp/` (SwiftUI host; its Xcode project is generated from `iosApp/project.yml` with XcodeGen). iOS (Stage 5: steps 1, 2 and 6 merged, step 4 playback done on `stage-5/playback`, step 5 downloads built on `stage-5/downloads`): `iosMain` holds the platform parts (Darwin HTTP engine, `IosPathProvider` = Application Support, `createIosDatabase` with the bundled SQLite driver, `KeychainCredentialStore`, `IosConnectivityMonitor` over `NWPathMonitor`, `IosAppInfo`, `IosPlexClientIdentifier` in user defaults, `IosDayZone` and `IosListeningClock` (Darwin `CLOCK_MONOTONIC`), `iosPlatformModule`) and the app shell: Swift calls `KoinIosKt.doInitKoin()`, `MainViewController` shows the shared `AppNavigation` (Home, Library, Search, Book details, Settings hub, Statistics) and sets the view controller's interface style from the theme choice (status bar, keyboard). The repository, sync and merging are the real shared ones. Since step 5 the iPhone build has downloads too (`iosDownloadModule`, see the Downloads on iOS paragraph) and `PlatformFeatures.IosFull` (`data/PlatformFeatures.kt`, Koin and `LocalPlatformFeatures`: playback and downloads on, car off, `downloadLocation` off so Settings shows no location choice); the step 2 stand-ins stay for tests: `notYetOnIosModule` (`di/KoinStart.kt`: `NotYetOnIosDownloadController` fails a start with "Downloads are not available on iOS yet", `NoLocalBookFiles`, `NoDownloadStorage`, `NoNotificationPermission`) with `PlatformFeatures.IosStreaming`, and `IosBrowsingOnly` with `NotYetOnIosPlaybackController` (starts fail with "Playback is not available on iOS yet"); with downloads off Book details disables Download with that text, there is no Downloads tab (`visibleTabs`), no Downloaded chip (`offeredChips`) or Download group in the Filter sheet, and the Downloads settings page keeps only the several-servers choices ("Books on several servers": Download from (only with downloads), Play from (when not downloaded), keep progress in sync; the two choice cards only with two or more servers; wording in `domain/settings/SourceChoiceText.kt`: a type line only when there is a server of it, server lines indented under their type when there are two or more of it; the hub's Downloads summary adds one short phrase such as "Download and play from Plex" unless both choices ask). The Android Auto row is hidden on iOS. `LocalPlatformFeatures` fails closed (`PlatformFeatures.None`) outside the provider in `AppNavigation`. Widgets have no iOS binding. `initKoin` starts what needs no player: the cover image loader, the Plex re-probe on network change, the library sync, `ServerProgressRefresher`, `PendingProgressFlusher`, `BookmarkSyncer`, the ABS history import, and `ForegroundSync` (`data/sync`: every server syncs at start, on `UIApplicationDidBecomeActiveNotification` observed from Kotlin and on a network change, at most every 2 minutes through `LibrarySyncCoordinator.syncAll`; offline it tries nothing and keeps the gap, and a pass missed or ended offline runs when the network returns; pull to refresh is never throttled; no background sync on iOS yet). Since step 4 `initKoin` also builds the player and starts the progress saver, live sync, mirror pusher, history and listening recorders. iOS playback (step 4): `iosPlaybackModule` binds `IosPlaybackController` (`iosMain/.../data/playback`), which is the common `EnginePlaybackController` (`commonMain/.../data/playback`, host-tested over a fake `AudioEngine`: one timeline over the files, skips 15/30 across files, rewind after a pause, sleep timer on a 500 ms ticker, session opened on load and closed on the next load, after 10 min paused (iOS keeps the book and the card) or when the files go, safe error texts) over `AvPlayerEngine` (an `AVQueuePlayer` holding the current and the next file for no gap; a seek into another file rebuilds the queue; a position asked for before an item is ready waits and playing waits for it; state polled every 200 ms only while something is pending (loading, a waiting position, a seek, playing asked for), plus the end, failure and stall notifications; a failure while paused and settled is seen at the next Play). Streamed audio gets the token as fixed per-asset headers (`AVURLAssetHTTPHeaderFieldsKey`, not a public key: fine for sideloading, review before the App Store) after `StreamAuthorizer` checked the address with one `Range: bytes=0-0` request on the probe client (no redirects followed): ABS 401 refreshes once through `AbsAuthorizer`, Plex 401 is "Sign in again", a redirect is followed only to the same scheme, host and port (`streamRedirectTarget`) else "The audio moved to another address". AVFoundation follows redirects itself with the headers, so a server that redirects only LATER range requests elsewhere is not caught (known limitation). A failed item is checked again and rebuilt where the engine stands at most once a minute (inside a UIKit background task), then the error shows and Play retries. Review fixes (PR #58, one wave): the ABS play request is per platform (`AbsPlayerProfile`, `platformAbsPlayer` expect/actual: Android sends exactly what it always did; iOS sends `forceDirectPlay=false`, `mediaPlayer=AVPlayer`, Apple as manufacturer and only types AVFoundation plays, no Ogg/Opus, so the server serves those books as HLS); a Plex book with an Ogg/Opus part fails with "This book's audio format cannot be played on this phone" (`applePlays`); a start that does not happen closes its new session at the session's own start position (also on Android's rollback paths), never at 0; after 10 min paused iOS closes only the server session and lets the audio session go (the book, its place and the lock screen card stay; Play loads it again with a new session; `idleAction`, re-checked when the timer fires so a late timer never undoes a Play); recovery runs in a UIKit background task (expiry pauses with "Playback paused while reconnecting", Play retries) and rebuilds where the engine stands now; the stream headers are renewed every 10 min and on each new file (an ABS token close to expiry is refreshed first) and the queued next file is rebuilt with them; a Plex 401 on a stream blanks the token; only 401 is refreshed (403 = "The server does not let this account play this book"); `AVURLAssetOverrideMIMETypeKey` (by its string, iOS 17+) and precise timing for MP3; a refused audio session shows "The audio is in use by another app" instead of buffering; a media services reset rebuilds the player; queue inserts are checked with `canInsertItem`; `headerPlayerModule` (common) carries the iOS player wiring so `KoinModulesTest` verifies it. `IosPlaybackController` adds the audio session (playback, spoken audio, long-form route sharing, activated before each start), interruptions (resume only if it was playing and the system says shouldResume, no rewind; `decideAfterInterruption`), route changes (headphones gone pause), `MPRemoteCommandCenter` (play, pause, toggle, skip 30/15 with preferred intervals, drag position, next/previous chapter; `remoteAction`, disabled without a book or with under two chapters) and the Now Playing card (title, author, chapter, length, position, speed; set again only on a change or a drift over 1.5 s, `nowPlayingNeedsUpdate`; no artwork). Pure rules in `domain/playback/AudioSessionRules.kt` and `data/playback/StreamAuthorizer.kt`/`AppleMediaErrors.kt` are tested in commonTest. `project.yml` has `UIBackgroundModes: audio` and links AVFoundation and MediaPlayer. Compiled and CI-linked only: never run on a simulator or a phone. `:shared:compileKotlinIosSimulatorArm64` type-checks commonMain and iosMain on Windows too (klib only); linking the framework and the Xcode build happen only in the macOS CI job. The iOS app has never run on a simulator or a phone (steps 2 and 4 are compiled and CI-linked only). iOS notes: the Darwin engine runs on a session with no URL cache and no cookies (responses carry tokens); Darwin ignores HttpTimeout's connect timeout (only request and socket ones), so downloads rely on the downloader's own stall check (60 s before the headers and between reads); downloaded files sit in a folder excluded from backup (`NSURLIsExcludedFromBackupKey`); a Keychain read that fails for another reason than "not found" throws (`KeychainUnavailableException`), the server list reads it as empty for now and a save never writes that empty value over the stored token; Keychain items left by an earlier install are deleted on the first launch (user-defaults marker); Info.plist has `CADisableMinimumFrameDurationOnPhone` (Compose's start check stops the app without it) and `NSAllowsArbitraryLoads` (fine for sideloading, an App Store build would need a narrower exception); an iOS refusal of the local network (-1009 "Local network prohibited") is `ProblemKind.LOCAL_NETWORK_OFF` and the Servers note says "Local network access may be off for ChapterPlayer in iOS Settings".
- Downloads on iOS (Stage 5 step 5; compiled and CI-linked only, NEVER run): `iosDownloadModule` (`iosMain/.../di/KoinIos.kt`) includes the common `inAppDownloadModule` (`di/InAppDownloadModule.kt`: the same downloader, sources, controller, length filler, queue restore and `RoomLocalBookFiles` over `DownloadFilesLocalDisk` as Android, plus the platform's files, `DownloadPlace`, scheduler and notification permission; `KoinModulesTest` verifies it and resolves the scheduler's parts, `DownloadSchedulerParts`, with host stand-ins; iOS passes only constructors of its own classes, which are compile-checked). Files: `IosDownloadFiles` (POSIX `open`/`ftruncate`/`write`/`F_FULLFSYNC` (fallback `fsync`)/`rename`, a full disk reads "ENOSPC"; `NSFileManager` for folders, listing and deletion; free space = `NSURLVolumeAvailableCapacityForImportantUsageKey`, else the file system's free size) under `<Application Support>/downloads` (`IosDownloadPlace`, label "This iPhone"; never purged; `NSURLIsExcludedFromBackupKey` set on the folder whenever it is created). iOS may move the app's data container on an update, so every stored path is read through `IosDownloadFiles.current` (`pathInContainer`, `domain/downloads/BackgroundDownloads.kt`), also by the player's file URLs. Scheduling: `InProcessDownloadScheduler` (common, tested in commonTest) runs each book's job in the app process with WorkManager's rules (one job per book, kept unless replaced; waits until `ConnectivityMonitor.downloadAllowed(wifiOnly)` holds, stops and saves when it no longer does, waits again; a "try again later" result reruns after `schedulerRetryDelayMs`, 30 s doubling to 5 h; `stopAll`). `ConnectivityMonitor.networkState` (online, metered) comes on iOS from `NWPathMonitor` (expensive or constrained = metered, no optimistic first value; one monitor shared by all collectors); Android does not read it. `IosDownloadScheduler` (iosMain, built from `DownloadSchedulerParts`) holds UIKit background time only while a download transfers with the app in the background and no continued task holds the app, and not again after it expired until the app was in front (`backgroundTimeStep`); on expiry it stops every job (bytes saved, books back to QUEUED) unless a continued task runs (`stopOnBackgroundTimeExpiry`) and gives the time back at once; `UIApplicationDidBecomeActiveNotification` runs the queue restore again (the launch runs it too). On iOS 26+ a download the user starts or resumes in the app (`DownloadScheduler.scheduleByUser`, called by the controller's enqueue and resume; the default is a plain `schedule`, so Android is unchanged; the foreground queue restore does not ask) asks for a `BGContinuedProcessingTask` (identifier `<bundle id>.downloads`, registered at launch and used only when Info.plist's `BGTaskSchedulerPermittedIdentifiers` lists it, so a re-signed bundle id falls back; strategy fail; title, progress line and per-mille progress from `continuedTaskText`; ends 5 s after nothing transfers; an accepted request that is never launched is forgotten after 30 s; its expiration handler (iOS, or the user's cancel in the system UI; one task covers the queue) hops to the main thread, ends the task once and stops the jobs only with the app in the background, `continuedTaskEnd`). A download that finishes or fails while the app is not in front posts a local notification (`downloadEndNotice`, `IosNotificationPermission` over `UNUserNotificationCenter`, asked once before the first download like Android). `project.yml`: `UIBackgroundModes` audio and processing, the permitted identifier, UserNotifications and Network linked, BackgroundTasks weakly linked. Known limits: the Darwin engine buffers a response without bound when the disk is slower than the network (not limited yet); continued processing is untested against iOS's real acceptance rules. The redirect `resolve()` in `FileDownloader.kt` is an RFC 3986 resolver, tested against java.net.URI (`RedirectResolveTest`)
